Connecting Apple Health

In Health Atlas, tap Add → Connect Apple Health and allow the categories you want to share. Your numbers then update by themselves when you open the app. To stop sharing, open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Health Atlas. For lab results your doctors shared through Health Records, you can also import a Health export file: in the Health app, tap your picture → Export All Health Data → Save to Files, then choose export.zip in Health Atlas.

Where is my data?

Your answers and Apple Health data never leave your iPhone. An account is optional. If you create one, it keeps your email, your username, your challenge records and your Atlas score, and what you choose to share with friends who added you back (your latest blood results, your record videos). You can switch each of those off, and delete the account with everything in it, under You → Your account. The privacy policy has every detail.
