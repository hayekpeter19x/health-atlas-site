Importing Apple Health

In the Health app, tap your picture → Export All Health Data → Save to Files. Then in Health Atlas, tap Add → Import from Apple Health and choose export.zip. Big exports can take a minute.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There’s no account. Deleting the app deletes your data. See the privacy policy.

Not medical advice
